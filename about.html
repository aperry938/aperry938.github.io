Researcher investigating how humans learn, move, and form relationships with intelligent systems. Three graduate degrees spanning kinesiology, exercise science, and computer science (AI/ML). U.S. Air Force combat veteran. Trilingual. Lived across five countries on four continents. Applying to PhD programs in Human-Centered AI.

Early Life & Military Service

Born at Fort Riley, Kansas and raised in a Bavarian village of 2,500 as the only mixed-race child. Navigating four distinct cultural environments before age 18—rural Bavaria, multicultural Kaiserslautern, an American military high school, and U.S. Air Force Basic Training—cultivated an observational capacity for human behavior that would later become a research orientation.

As a C-17 Aircraft Loadmaster, Anthony logged over 2,000 flight hours and 455 combat sorties across Operations Enduring Freedom, Iraqi Freedom, and New Dawn. He directed airlift for special forces combat insertions, aeromedical evacuations, dignified transfers of fallen service members, and presidential support missions for Barack Obama. His service earned the Air Medal for sustained meritorious aerial achievement and the Humanitarian Service Medal for Hurricane Sandy relief operations reaching 8.2 million people. These were formative years of first-person experience with high-stakes decision-making, systems management under uncertainty, and human performance at the limits of stress—experiences that raised questions about cognition and adaptation that existing frameworks could not answer.

Research Foundation

A European track & field qualifier and weightlifting club president, the physical dimension has always been central. At San Diego State University—graduating Cum Laude with Distinction in Kinesiology and Dean’s List honors—and California Baptist University (M.S., 3.87 GPA), Anthony studied how the body and brain acquire skill under conditions of stress and uncertainty.

His master’s thesis designed a two-arm randomized controlled trial investigating yoga intervention for overuse injury prevention in distance runners, involving multimodal data collection (EMG, goniometry, Oslo OSTRC questionnaire), full IRB protocol development, and a systematic review of 30+ peer-reviewed articles spanning biomechanics, neurophysiology, and motor control. The insight: static, analog interventions could not model the dynamic, non-linear fluctuations of human physiology in real time—a recognized limitation that catalyzed the pursuit of computational approaches.

Embodied Practice & Phenomenological Inquiry

A 500-hour Registered Yoga Teacher certified across Ashtanga, Vinyasa, Yin, Yoga Nidra, and Pranayama, completed during three years of intensive training in Bali. This represents a sustained first-person investigation of embodied experience and interoceptive awareness—a phenomenological method that complements and grounds computational inquiry. The lived practice of attention, proprioception, and breath regulation directly informs the design of AI systems that interact with human bodies and minds, making the phenomenological orientation genuine rather than merely academic.

Engineering & Current Work

Completing an M.S. in Computer Science (AI/ML) at Western Governors University, expected June 2026. Founded Meridian Labs: 10 production apps across 4 brands, 25,000+ questions, 8 proprietary algorithms grounded in 30+ peer-reviewed citations in cognitive science and testing psychology. The AI-augmented single-researcher workflow—where one person orchestrates LLMs for architecture design, content generation, and quality control—is itself a subject of inquiry into the future of human-AI collaboration.

Additional credentials include the AWS Certified Machine Learning Engineer—Associate, IBM Generative AI certification, and UC San Diego Discrete Mathematics specialization.
