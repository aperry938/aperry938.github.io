Research Interests

I investigate how adaptive technologies can be designed to engage not only with what people know, but with how they experience knowing, moving, and performing under pressure. My work integrates real-time computer vision for biomechanical analysis, metacognitive modeling in adaptive learning systems, and the phenomenological design of human–AI interaction—unified by a commitment to grounding computational methods in the lived reality of the people they serve. A defining question across my projects is how human–AI collaboration reshapes the experience of learning, creating, and making decisions: the process by which I build these systems is itself a site of inquiry into that question.
