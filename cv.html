Research and Projects

Pre-registered study of AI use and self-disclosure 2026
  • Designed, pre-registered (OSF osf.io/5xzjs) and ran a study (N = 55) comparing AI users and non-users on validated measures (Self-Disclosure Index, PRCA-12) plus two original psychometric scales with acceptable reliability
  • AI users were far more willing to self-disclose to a new acquaintance (d = 1.08), and 57% said they had sought professional help for something first raised with AI
her, a single-participant study of voice AI 2025
Preprint and data: doi:10.5281/zenodo.19144583
  • Recorded 68 voice sessions (83 hours) with GPT-4o, Gemini, Grok and Claude between September and December 2025, using structured games, debates and stress tests, and scored them on rubrics I designed
  • One participant, one rater and unvalidated scales, so the results can only suggest patterns; the preprint and the coded session data are released on Zenodo

Master’s thesis proposal on yoga and overuse injury in distance runners 2018
View Thesis Proposal (PDF)
  • Proposed a two-arm randomized controlled trial (N=50; 25 experimental, 25 control) with 12-week Hatha Yoga intervention to investigate overuse injury prevention in distance runners aged 22–35 with marathon experience
  • Conducted a literature review of 20 peer-reviewed articles spanning biomechanics, neuromuscular physiology, and motor control
  • Developed a multimodal data collection protocol integrating EMG, goniometry, postural analysis (Kent Grid), standardized ROM tests (Thomas, Ober’s, SLR), and the Oslo Sports Trauma Research Center Overuse Injury Questionnaire
  • Prepared full IRB submission with block randomization stratified by sex, allocation concealment, informed consent, and ethical oversight protocols per 45 C.F.R. 46
  • Formulated statistical analysis plan: 2×2 mixed-design ANOVA with a priori power analysis (G*Power, f=0.25, α=0.05, power=0.80), intent-to-treat with multiple imputation, and Bonferroni correction for family-wise error control
