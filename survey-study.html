Pre-registered study, 2026

Judgment-Free but Not Risk-Free

How perceived emotional safety with AI companions relates to human self-disclosure and help-seeking. A pre-registered (OSF osf.io/5xzjs) study comparing 30 people who use AI for personal conversation with 25 non-users on validated human-directed measures (Self-Disclosure Index, PRCA-12) and two original scales.

AI users were substantially more willing to self-disclose to a new acquaintance (d = 1.08), 57% reported seeking professional help for an issue first discussed with AI, and perceived emotional safety with AI predicted AI-context help-seeking (r = .51) but not interpersonal disclosure (r = .11). The sample was small and unbalanced by nationality, so the results are preliminary.

De-identified data and analysis scripts will be released with the paper.
