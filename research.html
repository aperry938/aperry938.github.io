Research Interests

My research investigates how adaptive AI systems can use physiological and behavioral signals to calibrate human-AI collaboration. I study how confidence calibration (the alignment between what people believe they know and what they actually know) can serve as a real-time adaptive signal, and how physiological sensing (EEG, HRV, EDA) can provide implicit measures of cognitive state to improve trust and decision quality in human-AI teams.

Three threads run through the current work. The first is production-scale adaptive AI with confidence-calibrated algorithms. The second examines how those algorithms generalize across professional domains. The third builds frameworks for integrating physiological biosignals (EEG, HRV, EDA) with adaptive systems to inform real-time cognitive state estimation.

Cross-Domain Analysis of a Confidence-Calibrated Adaptive Learning Engine

A. C. Perry

Submitted to EDM 2026 Poster/Demo Track (19th International Conference on Educational Data Mining, Seoul)

Analyzes how item bank properties interact with confidence-calibrated adaptation across four certification domains (35,250 items, 22 apps). A two-factor predictor computed from difficulty spread and base accuracy ranks misconception concentration across domains from item bank statistics alone, enabling cold-start domain screening for offline-first systems without server-side telemetry.

The Safety–Agency Inversion: Longitudinal Multi-Method Evidence from Frontier Voice AI Companions

A. C. Perry

Targeting IJHCI (International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction); arXiv preprint May 2026

Documents a safety–agency inversion in frontier AI voice companions: models with stronger safety alignment consistently exhibit lower relational agency across 68 sessions and 83+ hours of naturalistic interaction. Five behavioral measures converge across three methodologically independent evidence streams. Introduces the HERVAC evaluation framework and releases the Her Dataset.

Additional papers in preparation. Preprint links will be added as manuscripts are posted to arXiv.

Current Projects

Defense in Depth: Hardcoded Safety Architecture for AI Mental Health Systems

In progress — targeting AIES 2026

Evaluates a pre-AI hardcoded screening layer for crisis detection in AI systems processing mental health content. The architecture is immune to prompt injection, model drift, and API failures by design. Compares hardcoded pattern-matching against LLM-based classifiers using an adversarial test corpus, grounded in 357 lines of deployed production code from a sleep and dream wellness application.

Meridian Labs Platform

Ongoing — 22 production apps, 35,250+ items

Educational technology platform serving as both a commercial product and a research instrument. The adaptive engine’s six-algorithm cascade processes binary confidence judgments through asymmetric scoring, confidence-modulated spaced repetition, misconception detection, smart review, readiness prediction, and Bloom’s-aligned assessment across four professional certification domains. Privacy-preserving on-device storage enables fully offline operation.

Interactive AI/ML Learning Map

Deployed — 42 concepts, 7 interactive explorations

Knowledge-graph-driven adaptive learning platform for AI/ML concepts. Integrates Bayesian Knowledge Tracing, Ebbinghaus-calibrated spaced repetition, prerequisite-gated curriculum sequencing, and an open learner model exposing algorithm transparency to learners. Seven interactive explorations (gradient descent, neural networks, attention, clustering, decision boundaries, data preprocessing, reinforcement learning) run real computations in-browser. The platform’s integration of adaptive mastery tracking with interactive visualization and knowledge graph navigation is, to our knowledge, novel.
