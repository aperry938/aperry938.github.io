The Non-Linear Path

Origins

From the cargo hold of a C-17 over Afghanistan to a kinesiology lab in San Diego to the frontier of human-AI interaction — the thread connecting every chapter is the same question: how do humans learn and perform under pressure?

455 combat sorties taught me what no classroom covers about performing when the stakes are real. Graduate research in motor learning and cognitive science gave me the language to describe it. AI engineering gave me the tools to build systems around it.
