Body

ZENith

Computer Vision · Biomechanical Analysis

Biomechanical assessment system using pose estimation and a 98-video dataset with kinesiologist annotations. 30 domain-informed features achieve equivalent classification accuracy to 132 raw pose landmarks with 4× fewer dimensions. A VAE quality model validates against expert ratings (ρ=0.958). Full evaluation: ablation study, quality validation, and latency benchmarks under 1% of a 30fps frame budget.

Connection

“Her”

Human-AI Interaction · Affective Computing · Longitudinal Study

Longitudinal study of relational competence across frontier voice AI: 68 sessions, 83+ hours, four models. Documents a safety–agency inversion—models with stronger safety alignment consistently exhibit lower relational agency. Introduces the HERVAC evaluation framework (six dimensions) and the Nine Circles adversarial stress-testing protocol. Five behavioral measures converge across three methodologically independent evidence streams.

Wellness Technology · Adaptive AI · Sleep Science

Adaptive sleep and dream wellness system that personalizes to physiological and behavioral signals. Origins

From a Bavarian village to military aviation to a kinesiology lab to the frontier of human-AI interaction—each chapter has been shaped by the same question: how do humans learn, adapt, and make sense of complex environments?

Lived experience across cultures, disciplines, and continents revealed the limits of any single framework for understanding human cognition. Graduate research gave me the language. Computational tools gave me the means to build systems that meet people where they actually are.
