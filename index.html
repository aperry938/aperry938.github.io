AI & Human Communication

Pre-registered study, 2026

In a pre-registered study (N = 55) I compared people who talk to AI about personal matters with people who don't, on validated measures of self-disclosure and communication apprehension. AI users were far more willing to open up to a new acquaintance (d = 1.08), and 57% said they had sought professional help for something first raised with AI.

her

Single-participant study, 2025

Over four months in late 2025 I recorded 68 voice sessions (83 hours) with GPT-4o, Gemini, Grok and Claude and scored them on rubrics I designed. With one participant, one rater and unvalidated scales it can only suggest patterns, such as how readily GPT-4o gave up a position under pushback. The preprint and the coded session data are on Zenodo.

Meridian Labs

Adaptive learning platform, 28 apps

Educational technology platform I built and run: 28 apps across four certification fields, with 75,000+ practice questions. The adaptive engine weights each answer by the learner’s stated confidence, so confident mistakes come back soonest, with spaced repetition, misconception detection and timed exam practice. Described in a preprint. Built and maintained by one person, with heavy use of AI tooling across the product lifecycle.

Interactive AI Map

Interactive learning tool, 2025

Browser-based course map of 42 AI and machine-learning concepts on a force-directed knowledge graph. Tracks mastery with Bayesian Knowledge Tracing, shows learners the model’s estimates, schedules review with spaced repetition, and includes seven interactive explorations that run real computations in the browser. Not yet evaluated with learners.

ZENith

Computer vision, 2025

Real-time yoga-form feedback from a webcam. Thirty hand-designed joint-angle features classify 10 poses as accurately as 132 raw pose landmarks, and a variational autoencoder trained on correct form separates good from poor form in a 100-video self-recorded dataset (98 used after quality screening), with inference under 1% of a 30 fps frame budget. No external raters yet.

Somnia.ai

Dream journal app, 2025

Sleep and dream journal with AI analysis. Dream entries are analyzed by Gemini through a server proxy after a keyword screen for crisis language, with offline queuing. Voice-to-text capture, generated audio (binaural beats, brown noise) via the Web Audio API, and pattern tracking across mood, themes and sleep quality. Journal data syncs to a server account.
