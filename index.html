Human-AI Interaction

her

HCI · Affective Computing · Longitudinal Study

Longitudinal study of relational competence across frontier voice AI: 68 sessions, 83+ hours, four models. Documents a safety–agency inversion: models with stronger safety alignment consistently exhibit lower relational agency. Introduces the HERVAC evaluation framework (six dimensions) and the Nine Circles adversarial stress-testing protocol. Five behavioral measures converge across three methodologically independent evidence streams.

Communication Research

AI & Human Communication

Human Subjects Research · Psychometrics · Pre-Registered

Pre-registered cross-sectional survey (N=55) examining how AI companion use relates to interpersonal communication and help-seeking. Found that AI users show substantially higher willingness to self-disclose to humans (d=1.08), with 57% seeking professional help for issues first discussed with AI. A key dissociation: perceived emotional safety with AI predicts help-seeking but not interpersonal disclosure—suggesting AI functions as behavioral rehearsal, not emotional transfer.

Computer Vision

ZENith

Computer Vision · Biomechanical Analysis

Biomechanical assessment system using pose estimation and a 100-video dataset with kinesiologist annotations. 30 domain-informed features achieve equivalent classification accuracy to 132 raw pose landmarks with 4× fewer dimensions. A VAE quality model validates against expert ratings (ρ=0.958). Full evaluation: ablation study, quality validation, and latency benchmarks under 1% of a 30fps frame budget.

Origins

Grew up in a small Bavarian village, enlisted in the U.S. Air Force at twenty, and spent four years as a C-17 Loadmaster in Operation Enduring Freedom, Operation Iraqi Freedom, and Operation New Dawn. After separating, studied kinesiology at SDSU and CBU, then spent several years living and working remotely across Costa Rica, Indonesia, Peru, and Vietnam.

Each stage pointed toward the same set of questions: how humans learn, how they interact with AI systems, and what happens when those systems get the relationship wrong.
